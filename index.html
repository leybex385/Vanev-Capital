THE FIRM

About Vanev Capital

Vanev Capital is an investment and strategic management firm focused on artificial intelligence, innovative capital, and long-term value creation.

The company is committed to connecting capital, technology, data, talent, and real-world industry needs through investment, research, and strategic resource integration, identifying and supporting companies and innovative projects with long-term development potential.

Our philosophy is not simply to seek short-term returns, but to focus on whether a company can truly establish sustainable competitive advantages.

For potential investments and strategic partnerships, Vanev Capital evaluates not only financial performance and market valuation, but also technology capabilities, business models, management teams, organizational efficiency, market opportunities, and long-term capital returns.

Fabrice Fiol

Board Advisor / Member of the Strategic Advisory Committee
Strategic Capital Advisor | Vanev Capital

Fabrice Fiol is a senior expert in global alternative assets and long-term institutional capital. He currently serves as Managing Director of Risk Management for the Insurance Solutions division at Blue Owl Capital. Blue Owl Capital is a leading global alternative asset management firm, with more than $300 billion in assets under management, specializing in private credit, insurance capital asset allocation, and long-term asset investment.

During his tenure at Blue Owl, he has been responsible for the comprehensive risk management and asset-liability assessment of insurance capital investment portfolios, developing deep expertise in the investment decision-making frameworks, asset due diligence standards, and risk-pricing methodologies of insurance capital and long-term institutional investors. Prior to joining Blue Owl, he served as Managing Director at Kuvare Asset Management, where he led the development of alternative asset risk management frameworks. He also served as a lecturer at Columbia University School of Professional Studies, where he conducted long-term research on long-term capital allocation and private credit risk models.

As a Strategic Capital Advisor to Vanev Capital, Fabrice Fiol draws on decades of experience in asset assessment and risk architecture design for large-scale long-term capital to provide strategic guidance on Vanev Capital’s investment research system, long-term project value assessment framework, and capital structure optimization for portfolio companies. From the perspective of global institutional investors, he assists the team in refining underlying asset risk identification mechanisms, strengthening value narratives designed for long-term capital, and supporting Vanev Capital in identifying and developing companies in the artificial intelligence and emerging technology sectors that demonstrate the ability to create sustainable long-term value.
