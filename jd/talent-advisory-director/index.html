Executive Talent & Strategic Advisory

Senior Talent & Advisory Director

Dallas, TX | Hybrid / Remote Options Available

Role Overview

Lead C-suite & executive search for portfolio firms, build talent strategy and manage the global industry expert network.

Key Responsibilities

  • Partner intimately with portfolio founders to define executive hiring needs and long-term organizational strategy.
  • Execute end-to-end leadership searches for VP, C-level, and Board Member positions across the portfolio.
  • Cultivate, expand, and manage Vanev Capital's proprietary network of industry advisors and operating partners.
  • Advise portfolio companies on organizational design, culture building, and equity compensation strategies.
  • Represent Vanev Capital in the broader talent ecosystem, building the firm's employer brand.

Required Qualifications

  • 8+ years of executive search or internal talent acquisition experience, strictly focusing on technology and executive leadership roles.
  • Exceptional, active network of senior executives and founders in the global tech ecosystem.
  • Strong technical understanding of equity compensation, cap tables, and venture capital structures.
  • High EQ, outstanding relationship management skills, and executive presence.

Preferred Qualifications & Skills

  • Experience working within a top-tier retained executive search firm or as an in-house talent partner at a leading VC/PE firm.
