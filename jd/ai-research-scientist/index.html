AI Research Scientist (Financial Vertical)

Dallas, TX | Hybrid / Remote Options Available

Role Overview

Develop and fine-tune finance-focused AI models, conduct time-series market data mining, factor discovery and model knowledge distillation & alignment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and implement machine learning architectures for financial forecasting and asset evaluation.
  • Discover and backtest predictive alpha factors using large-scale alternative and market datasets.
  • Perform model knowledge distillation to deploy lightweight, low-latency models for real-time analysis.
  • Align foundational AI models with proprietary financial reasoning frameworks.
  • Collaborate with the investment teams to integrate quantitative signals into fundamental research.

Required Qualifications

  • Ph.D. or Master's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related quantitative field.
  • Deep expertise in deep learning, natural language processing (NLP), and time-series analysis.
  • Strong proficiency in Python and deep learning frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow).
  • Solid understanding of financial markets and quantitative modeling.

Preferred Qualifications & Skills

  • Published research in top-tier AI/ML conferences (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR).
  • Experience with financial datasets, quantitative trading strategies, or algorithmic execution.
