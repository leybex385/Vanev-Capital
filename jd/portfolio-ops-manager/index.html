Portfolio Operations Manager

Dallas, TX | Hybrid / Remote Options Available

Role Overview

Support portfolio companies with organizational optimization, operational transformation and AI implementation, track key business KPIs.

Key Responsibilities

  • Act as a dedicated operational partner and advisor to portfolio company founders and executives.
  • Identify operational bottlenecks and implement process optimizations to accelerate growth.
  • Guide portfolio companies through enterprise AI integration, digital transformation, and tooling upgrades.
  • Establish and continuously monitor standardized KPI reporting across the entire investment portfolio.
  • Facilitate knowledge sharing and best practices among portfolio company operators.

Required Qualifications

  • 5-8 years of experience in management consulting, corporate strategy, or startup operations (e.g., Chief of Staff, BizOps).
  • Strong track record of executing operational change and driving measurable efficiency improvements.
  • Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and financial modeling skills.
  • High emotional intelligence and the ability to influence executive teams without direct authority.

Preferred Qualifications & Skills

  • Experience operating within a venture-backed technology company.
  • Familiarity with modern AI tools, automation workflows, and enterprise SaaS platforms.
